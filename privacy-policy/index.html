Privacy Policy for Canopy Dash

Effective date: October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the developer of Canopy Dash ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the Canopy Dash Android game (the "App").

1. Information handled by the App

Canopy Dash does not require an account. We do not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, or files through the App.

Your game progress and settings, such as coins, unlocked items, missions, achievements, and high scores, are stored locally on your device. We do not operate a server that receives this gameplay progress. Local data can be removed by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App.

2. Advertising and automatically collected information

The App uses Unity LevelPlay for advertising mediation. Advertising providers may include Unity/ironSource and Google AdMob. These third-party SDKs may automatically collect or receive:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate a device's general location;
  • device and advertising identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID;
  • App and ad interactions, including App launches, taps, ad impressions, and video views;
  • device, operating system, language, and network information; and
  • diagnostic and performance information, such as crash, loading, and SDK performance data.

These providers may use the information to deliver contextual or personalized ads where permitted, measure ad performance, limit repeated ads, perform analytics, prevent fraud and abuse, and maintain or improve their services. Information may be shared with advertising demand partners and service providers as described in the providers' policies.

3. Advertising choices and consent

Where required by law, the App requests your advertising privacy choices before requesting ads. Depending on your choice and region, ads may be personalized or non-personalized. You may reopen available choices from the App's Settings > Privacy > Ad Privacy Choices. You may also reset or delete your Android advertising ID from your device's privacy settings.

Withdrawing consent does not necessarily remove all ads; it may result in contextual or limited ads.

4. Third-party privacy policies

  • Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google advertising controls

5. Legal bases and international transfers

Where applicable, advertising providers may process information based on consent, legitimate interests, contractual necessity, or legal obligations, as explained in their policies. These providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Their policies describe the safeguards they use for international transfers.

6. Data retention and security

Local game progress remains on your device until you clear the App's data or uninstall it. Third-party advertising providers determine their own retention periods as described in their policies. Network data sent by Google Mobile Ads is encrypted in transit using TLS. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. Children

Canopy Dash is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly ask children to submit personal information directly to us. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to us, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing, or to withdraw consent. Because we do not operate user accounts or receive your locally stored game progress, we may not possess data that identifies you. Requests concerning data processed by an advertising provider may need to be submitted directly to that provider using the links above.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, its advertising partners, or legal requirements change. We will update the effective date when changes are made.
